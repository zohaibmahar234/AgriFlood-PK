## Abstract
AgriFlood-PK is an academic geospatial prototype for Khairpur District, Sindh that separates forecast-based flood advisory, satellite-observed inundation, potential agricultural exposure, and vegetation recovery. The system integrates Open-Meteo/GloFAS with a Google Earth Engine workflow using Sentinel-1, Sentinel-2, ESA WorldCover, JRC Global Surface Water and SRTM. The baseline deliberately remains interpretable and is not described as a trained model or calibrated probability. Quantitative scientific results are pending authenticated Earth Engine execution and acquisition of a time-matched reference layer; software verification results are reported separately.
